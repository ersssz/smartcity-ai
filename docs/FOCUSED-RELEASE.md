# Focused discovery and walking release

Authority: the user's explicit scope reduction and subsequent requests for interesting/historical places, real photographs, weather and holiday recommendations on 2026-10-20. The earlier full beta plan remains a deferred backlog.

## User outcome

After a short welcome and city reveal, the visitor sees what is worth exploring in Astana. Photo cards explain why each place is interesting. Selecting one highlights its actual public location. A historical filter gives a clear way into the old city. About the city retains the district guide.

The existing prepared Bayterek walk remains the supported guided route. Other place cards must show their actual position and an explicitly labeled external-map action; they must not silently open the Bayterek route. A route is supported only after its geometry and sources are integrated and checked.

Stories, narration and durable reading progress form the main walk. Quests can be skipped without inventing an answer. Reading completion is separate from physical arrival and rewards. Previous progress and the legacy manual diagnostic step remain stored.

## Context recommendations

- Fresh city-level weather can affect ordering and concise reasons. Adverse wind, cold and precipitation take priority over sunshine. Current model conditions do not guarantee the weather for an entire future walk.
- Show observation/model time, source and an explicit unavailable or stale state. Manual conditions are labeled as the visitor's choice, never measured weather.
- Use Astana's calendar date for sourced holidays. Differentiate the holiday itself, transferred days off, upcoming dates and confirmed events. Do not invent a festival or venue opening.
- Unknown indoor hours require checking before travel. Recommend no unverified shelter as guaranteed open.

## Verification required for delivery

Check strict types, meaningful persistence/context regressions, photograph identity and attribution, an actual APK build and installed version/hash. Inspect the real phone screen where accessible. Record unperformed playback, field, entrance, accessibility and platform checks separately. A built APK is not proof of an audible narration or a walk completed outside.

The continuation adopts the native background narration host in0.6.1. Actual API31 Home/background and own-app controls are checked; locked-screen, notification transport, newer Android and field gates remain separate. See `docs/delivery/NARRATION-0.6.1.md` for the exact installed artifact and evidence.

Deferred: public sharing/accounts, collection and achievement UI, GPS rewards, revision negotiation, full download packs and full-beta acceptance. Staged implementations remain available for later review.
